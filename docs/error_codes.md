- `not_authenticated`
- `phone_not_verified`
- `event_not_open`
- `event_full`
- `request_already_exists`
- `request_not_found`
- `invalid_decision`
- `host_cannot_request_own_event`
- `nudge_rate_limit_daily`
- `nudge_cooldown_pair`
- `recaptcha_required`
- `block_required`
- `invalid_block`
- `cohorts_required`
- `invalid_cohort`
- `invalid_sport_sub`
- `no_block_nearby`
- `block_or_coords_required`
- `peer_not_found`
- `location_required`
- `title_required`
- `reason_required`
- `wire_to_aki_pipeline`
